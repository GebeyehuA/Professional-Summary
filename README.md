# Professional Summary
# Dr. Gebeyehu Abebe

**Assistant Professor | Remote Sensing Researcher | Geospatial data Scientist**

I am an Assistant Professor specializing in **Remote Sensing** and geospatial data analysis. My academic work focuses on advancing Earth observation methodologies, satellite image processing, and spatial modeling to address complex environmental and societal challenges. To date, I have authored and co-authored **over 30 peer-reviewed research publications** in international journals and conference proceedings.

Alongside my research, I am deeply committed to capacity building and open science. I regularly serve as a **Lead Instructor and Resource Person** for specialized workshops, faculty development programs, and technical training sessions, equipping researchers, students, and professionals with practical workflows in geospatial scripting and reproducible remote sensing analysis.
